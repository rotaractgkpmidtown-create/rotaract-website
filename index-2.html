WHO WE ARE

Gorakhpur ke young changemakers

We are a community of young entrepreneurs, chartered accountants, professionals and family-business owners — brought together by one belief: our city deserves our time, not just our talk.

As the Rotaract wing of the Rotary Club of Gorakhpur Midtown, we combine the century-old spirit of Rotary service with the energy and business acumen of a new generation.

OUR CITY

Rooted in Gorakhpur

From the spiritual aura of the Gorakhnath Mandir to the timeless pages of Geeta Press — this city has always given to the world. Now its young generation gives back to the city.

  • City of the Gorakhnath temple & the Nath tradition
  • Home of Geeta Press — printing wisdom since 1923
  • Gateway of Purvanchal — trade, terai & new enterprise
  • And now: home of Purvanchal's most driven Rotaract club
